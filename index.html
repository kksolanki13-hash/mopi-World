About the Universe

MOPi WORLD is a next-generation Play-to-Earn (P2E) Country-Simulator where players manage "cosmic bean" citizens known as MOPis. earn reward coin by complit the task. Unlike typical games, MOPi WORLD operates on an accelerated timeline (1 Earth Year = 1,008 MOPi Years), allowing for rapid evolution of empires and technologies.

Goals of the P2E Game

Sustainable Tokenomics

Earn reward coins by completing daily tasks and milestones, designed with anti-inflationary staking controls.

Rapid Empire Evolution

Harness the accelerated timeline (1 Earth Year = 1,008 MOPi Years) to grow technology, build alliances, and expand across galaxies.

Cosmic Bean Governance

Govern and direct your MOPis. Assign roles, allocate resources, and customize stats to optimize productivity.

Dynamic NFT Marketplace

Trade unique MOPi character variants, skins, and technology cards securely on the decentralized marketplace.

Future Roadmap

Our multi-year developmental timeline outlining milestones at 6-month intervals.

2026 H1

Genesis Mint

Developing 2D pixel-based NFTs, Smart Contracts deployment, and community collection dynamics. Release of 8,888 Genesis MOPis.

2026 H2

2D Evolution

Transition development from 2D pixel styles into fully interactive 3D assets. Expanding character traits and customization.

2027 H1

3D Minting

Initial distribution of the 3D NFT assets, expanding classes (Cyber Mage, Bio Hunter) and starting pre-alpha arena tests.

2027 H2

Economic Integration

Launching decentralized economy tokenomics, market smart contracts, and integrating assets directly into the game ecosystem.

2028 H1

Game Dev & Beta

Creation of the MOPi universe simulator. Full game systems implementation, tournament frameworks, and closed beta access.
